PIXELSLIME POD: MAKERFABS BUILD GUIDE
====================================

BOARD
-----
Makerfabs ESP32-S3 SPI TFT with Touch 3.5" ILI9488
https://www.makerfabs.com/esp32-s3-spi-tft-with-touch-ili9488.html

Board dimensions: 66 x 84.3 x 12 mm
Power: USB-C, 5 V
Display: 320 x 480 after rotation to portrait orientation

PROPOSED ENCLOSURE
------------------
External dimensions: 98 x 130 x 22 mm
Material: opaque PLA
Front panel: 2.5 mm
Rear cover: 2 mm
Board mounting: four M2.5 x 6 mm standoffs
Rear cover: four M2.5 screws
Buttons: three 12 mm holes

Do not finalize the STL before measuring the received board with calipers.
The manufacturer's dimensions do not include every connector and mounting-hole
position.

BUTTON WIRING
-------------
J3 is the black 1 x 15 expansion header on the rear of the board. The following
pinout was checked against the official Eagle schematic, revision 1.1.

J3-2  GND   -> common return for all three buttons
J3-10 GPIO6 -> RIGHT button
J3-11 GPIO4 -> LEFT button
J3-12 GPIO5 -> OPEN button

Each button is a normally open momentary contact:

GPIO ---- button ---- GND

No external resistor is required when the firmware uses INPUT_PULLUP. A released
button reads HIGH and a pressed button reads LOW.

BOARD-SIDE CONNECTION
---------------------
1. Separate four male-to-female Dupont wires.
2. Insert the male pins into J3-2, J3-10, J3-11 and J3-12.
3. Cut the female ends and solder the wires to the button terminals.
4. Cover every terminal with heat-shrink tubing.
5. Leave 10 to 12 cm of wire so the rear cover can be opened.
6. Join the three button returns to the wire connected to J3-2/GND.

BUTTON PARTS
------------
- Three normally open 12 mm panel-mount momentary buttons
- Four 2.54 mm male-to-female Dupont wires
- Heat-shrink tubing
